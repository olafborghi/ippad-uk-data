About

The IP-PAD project

IP-PAD, short for Interdisciplinary Perspectives on the Politics of Adolescence and Democracy, is a Doctoral Network funded by the European Union under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions. It studies how the cognitive, emotional and social changes of adolescence and early adulthood shape the way young people process political information and engage with politics.

As part of this work, the network surveys young people in Austria, Greece, the Netherlands, Poland and the United Kingdom, using a common set of questions and inviting respondents back in later waves. This website presents the UK data, which were collected by the team at Royal Holloway, University of London, with fieldwork by Gallup International and Survation. The data page describes how the surveys were carried out.

Partners

The network is formed by five universities.

  • University of Amsterdam, Netherlands
  • University of Vienna, Austria
  • Panteion University of Social and Political Sciences, Greece
  • Jagiellonian University, Poland
  • Royal Holloway, University of London, United Kingdom

More about the network and its other work is available at www.ippad.eu.

This website and the combined data

The combined UK data file and this website were prepared by Olaf Borghi at Royal Holloway, University of London. Questions about either are welcome at olaf.borghi@rhul.ac.uk (send an email).
